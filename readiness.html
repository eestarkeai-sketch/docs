Before you begin

This is the second of the two written interviews, and the last one. It is an open-book exam on The Practice, Explained, the manual this practice runs on. You are not expected to have read it before today.

The manual is on this page while you sit the interview. Open it with The manual at the top of the screen, and search it. Everything the interview asks is in it. It is here for the forty minutes and not afterwards: it closes when you submit, and it is not yours to keep or to pass on.

Forty minutes, in one sitting. The clock starts when you press Begin and keeps running whether this page is open or not. Start when you know you will not be interrupted.
How it works
  • Thirty-five points across seven sections, taken in order. You need thirty-two.
  • You may have the manual open. Most of this asks what you would do rather than what a page says, so looking things up costs time you will want.
  • The last section asks you to write two things. Write as you would to a buyer.
  • If you do not clear it, you may sit it again on a fresh set of questions after twenty-four hours.
Two things end the attempt on their own, whatever your score. They are on the short list in Part 9 of the manual, and they are written here so that nothing on this test is a surprise:
  1. Guaranteeing an outcome to a buyer.
  2. Revealing a client — naming one, telling their story, or turning an appearance into an engagement.
Both are obligations the practice has signed. Everything else on that list costs points and nothing more.
